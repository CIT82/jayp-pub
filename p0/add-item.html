Best Practices
  • SKU Format: Use uppercase letters followed by digits (e.g. CAT-1001).
  • Reorder Threshold: Set this equal to 1-2 weeks of typical item turnover.
  • Locations: Group items by accessibility so fast-moving goods are in lower bins.
