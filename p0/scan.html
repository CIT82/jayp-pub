Camera Scanning Tips

  • Good Lighting: Ensure the barcode is well-lit and avoid harsh glare or shadows on reflective labels.
  • Distance & Focus: Hold your phone 4–8 inches away from the product, keeping the camera steady until the code is read.
  • Supported 1D Formats: UPC-A, UPC-E, EAN-13, and Code 128 barcodes.
  • Local Product Database: Scanned items are stored permanently in browser localStorage, building an offline-ready product catalog.
