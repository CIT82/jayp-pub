About The Startup Club

The Startup Club is a community for students interested in entrepreneurship, business, technology, and turning creative ideas into real-world projects. Whether you already have a business idea or are simply curious about entrepreneurship, everyone is welcome.

What We Do

Our club brings students together to learn, share ideas, and build practical skills. Meetings include workshops, guest speakers, networking opportunities, and discussions about starting and growing a business.

Who Can Join?

No business experience is required. The Startup Club is open to students of all majors and experience levels. If you enjoy creating, learning, meeting new people, or exploring new opportunities, we'd love to have you join us.
